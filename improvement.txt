Bullet 3: Observability and latency
What works
Telemetry and reproducible evaluation are important AI-platform signals.
Citation and refusal correctness provide meaningful evaluation coverage.
CI integration demonstrates production-style engineering.
What weakens it

A 29-second p95 end-to-end latency is not a positive resume metric unless the project processes unusually long documents, runs entirely local models, or uses a deliberate multi-stage evaluation pipeline.

A recruiter reading this will likely interpret it as:

Five percent of requests take at least 29 seconds.

That is too slow for an interactive RAG application.

The phrase “explicitly track pipeline performance” also describes functionality rather than impact. And “CI/CD-ready” is weaker than saying the evaluation actually runs in CI.

What to do

Break the latency into stages:

Authentication and authorization
Elasticsearch retrieval
pgvector retrieval
Result fusion
LLM generation
Citation validation
LLM-as-a-judge evaluation

The 29 seconds may include synchronous evaluation or judge calls that should not be on the user-facing request path. If so, move those checks to an offline evaluation pipeline.

For the resume, either:

Omit the 29-second number and emphasize instrumentation and CI evaluation, or
Optimize latency and report the improved number.
Verdict

Engineering value: 8/10
Current resume impact: 5.5/10
